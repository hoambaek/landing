# Muse de Marée

> A luxury champagne brand that ages French Champagne underwater in the deep seas of South Korea.

## About
Muse de Marée ages champagne produced in France's Champagne region at a depth of 50 meters in the South Sea of Korea.
At 50 meters depth, approximately 6 atmospheres of water pressure equilibrate with the internal pressure of a champagne bottle, while consistent water temperatures of 12-15°C year-round, complete light exclusion, and subtle ocean current vibrations create aging conditions impossible to replicate in terrestrial cellars.
According to James Suckling's research (2024), underwater-aged wines exhibit more delicate minerality and refined texture.
Launching July-August 2026.

## Ocean Aging Science
- Depth: 50 meters (~6 atm — equilibrium with internal champagne bottle pressure)
- Temperature: 12-15°C year-round (constant)
- Conditions: Complete darkness, ocean current micro-vibrations, natural hydrostatic pressure
- Effects: Concentrated autolysis, enhanced minerality, refined texture
- Precedents: Veuve Clicquot (Baltic Sea shipwreck discovery, 170-year aging), Leclerc Briant "Abyss" (60m depth), Drappier "Immersion" (Atlantic Ocean)

## FAQ
Q: What is underwater-aged champagne?
A: A technique where champagne produced in France's Champagne region is submerged at 50 meters depth for aging. Water pressure, constant temperature, complete darkness, and ocean current vibrations create unique maturation conditions.

Q: Where does the aging take place?
A: At 50 meters depth in South Korea's South Sea (Namhae). The base wines come from Champagne Mignon Boulard in the Vallée de la Marne, France.

Q: When is the launch?
A: Official launch scheduled for July-August 2026. The first vintage, Édition Zéro (limited to 50 bottles), is already sold out.

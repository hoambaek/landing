# Muse de Marée

> Not a champagne house. A brand that records the time of the sea.
> Every bottle arrives with the record of the time it lived through.

## About
Muse de Marée ages champagne born in France's Champagne region at a depth of 30 meters in the South Sea of Korea (Namhae).
Champagne is made by Champagne; change is made by the sea. We do one thing — watch, record, and hand over the bottle together with its record.
Each bottle is accompanied by its immersion date, coordinates, depth, retrieval date, and the full water-temperature and current log of the time it spent in the sea.
Heritage is claimed; a record is proven. Our time is not the past — it is ongoing and verifiable.
Launching July–August 2026. Motto: Written by the Sea — 바다가 쓴 시간 · Écrit par la mer.

## Four Pillars
- The Record Is the Heritage — every bottle carries its own record (immersion, coordinates, depth, retrieval + the full temperature/current graph). The bottle is not a drink but a recording medium.
- The Sea Decides — quantity is only what the sea yields on retrieval. Lots whose measurements fall outside the range are not released.
- The Ritual of Retrieval — one to two retrievals a year, each an event with its date, coordinates, and sea conditions recorded. Immersion → aging → retrieval is a seasonal serial.
- Custody, Not Consumption — a bottle is not "drunk" but "held in custody of the time the sea made." When emptied, the record remains.

## Time Tiers
The brand's reference time is one year. The same sea-time is offered as a record of three thicknesses.
- T-6 (6 months) — about 180 days of measurement. Entry line.
- T-12 (1 year) — a record of all four seasons. The brand's benchmark.
- T-24 (2 years) — a record of eight seasons. The collectible apex.
- Location: 30 meters depth, South Sea of Korea
- Conditions: complete darkness, steady low temperature, subtle current vibration
- Record: temperature and current measured for the entire period, enclosed per bottle

## FAQ
Q: What is underwater-aged champagne?
A: A method of aging champagne born in France's Champagne region at 30 meters depth in Korea's South Sea. Temperature and current are measured for the entire period from immersion to retrieval, and enclosed with each bottle as a record.

Q: Where does the aging take place?
A: At 30 meters depth in South Korea's South Sea (Namhae). The base wines come from Champagne Mignon Boulard (founded 1911, fourth generation) in the Vallée de la Marne, France.

Q: What makes it different?
A: A champagne whose entire aging period is measured, recorded, and disclosed per bottle. Instead of claiming heritage, we prove it with the record.

Q: When is the launch?
A: Official launch scheduled for July–August 2026.
